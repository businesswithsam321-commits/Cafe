Our Story

Baked the slow way, on purpose

Bean & Batter started as a single oven and a folding table. Today it's still run by the same two hands that started it — we mill, ferment and bake in small batches, every single day, because that's the only way we know how to do it well.

No shortcuts, no packet mixes, no rushed proofs. Just good flour, good time, and a cafe that feels like your kitchen.
