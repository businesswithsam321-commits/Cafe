Our Comic Story

Where Real Food Meets 2D Illustration

Founded in 2021 by Chef Arjun Patel and illustrator Maya Rao, Sketch Cafe began as a passion project in a 400-sq-ft corner of Indiranagar. What started as weekend pop-ups serving hand-drawn menu boards quickly became Bangalore's most Instagrammable dining destination. Our signature 2D optical illusion interior — custom-painted tables, chairs, and walls with thick black ink borders — turns every meal into a living comic panel. We're known for our Comic Crust Pizza (wild mushrooms, buffalo mozzarella, balsamic glaze) and Ink Drip Hotcakes (buttermilk stack, forest berries, maple). Every dish is plated to pop against the monochrome surroundings, because we believe food should look as extraordinary as it tastes.

Why customers choose us:

  • Mind-bending 2D photo opportunities inside our real space.
  • Premium culinary standard menu items prepared freshly.
  • High-conversion ordering support across all platforms.
